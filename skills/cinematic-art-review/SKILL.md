# Cinematic Art Review

Make the creative work stronger before delivery, using what the actual images communicate. Do not certify your own output from its prompt or describe receiving feedback as model training. The aim is ambitious direction with photographic credibility, rather than merely removing defects.

## Establish what can change

Read the project's entry point, current brief, revision/status and relevant user feedback. Distinguish accepted components, unreviewed proposals and frozen approvals. Preserve liked identities, composition and authored design unless the current request changes them. A request to create a review skill does not itself request a remake of every existing film.

For Daily Cinematic Lab, read ART_DIRECTION.md, applicable LESSONS.md, the relevant inspected benchmark and project-specific face anchor. Read [references/creative-lead-taste.md](references/creative-lead-taste.md) for the dated starting taste record; current project direction wins. Production review uses only the verified approved foundation and session derivatives, excluding discarded drafts. Preparation review may inspect prior examples for learning.

## Review at the points where decisions matter

For environment preparation and revisions, follow [environment-continuity](../environment-continuity/SKILL.md): nine consistent individual camera views and one large 3×3 raster artwork per scene environment, with a plan and camera index. This standing user direction applies to new preparation; frozen approved packets stay unchanged.

During preparation, challenge the concept before committing assets; inspect the first face/environment assets before propagating them; review the selected sequence before final export. Resume completed stages rather than generating them again for a ceremonial review. For a review-only request, inspect and report; for authorised preparation/revision, also execute worthwhile reversible fixes within that scope.

For detailed marked corrections, translate the user's current legend into regional operations: preserve, build/fill, reduce or remove. Give added detail a structural, material, cultural or expressive purpose; check physical fit and visual weight, not detail density alone. Resolve the consequential junction/property in an actual close view and check another revealing angle where needed before spreading unresolved design into many derivatives. Verify the requested property in pixels: a prompted glass indicator can still be empty linework, and a corrected front can still omit the profile mechanism. Preserve in-flight intermediates when steering changes; this is a sequencing improvement within authorised preparation, not a new approval checkpoint. See the dated Ren evidence in references/creative-lead-taste.md.

View actual files, not only a contact sheet. Compare the anchor, setup, intermediate state and payoff at delivery size; inspect relevant face/material/contact details at native resolution. A close portrait cannot establish wide-shot fidelity. Record inspected paths and any unseen assets. Read the action and camera plan alongside the images, then judge what a viewer can understand without reading that plan.

Consider these questions together, giving more attention to the ones that affect this film:

- **Story surprise:** What does the setup make us expect? What actually happens? Which visible action changes our understanding? Can it read within the story-led proposed runtime? An impressive fulfilment of the setup is not automatically a reversal. Avoid making every surprise a threat, transformation or scale reveal.
- **Visual surprise:** What is distinctive about this world, silhouette, material, fashion or staging even before the payoff? Does a hybrid change how the world works, or merely place a futuristic prop beside an old costume? Quiet scenes can have bold material or functional invention without adding spectacle. Do not impose a futuristic genre on unrelated concepts.
- **Photographic credibility:** Check skin, anatomy, fabric, vegetation, metal/glass response, scale, contact shadows, exposure and lens depth independently of detail/sharpness. Find concrete synthetic cues and their locations. Preserve purposeful colour and clean future materials; dirt, blur, grain, flattened light and global desaturation are not universal realism fixes.
- **Identity and styling:** Compare actual anatomy, hairline, silhouette, signature makeup, ornaments and wardrobe against the selected anchor. Account for angle, expression and face pixel budget. Do not call unresolved detail either confirmed continuity or definite drift. Keep distinct characters within the intended design family; never infer ethnicity from a real reference person's image.
- **Sequence and world logic:** Track who holds what, contact, object count, scale, stage of deployment, empty-space proof, camera geography and light/shadow causality. A beautiful endpoint can contradict the action. Specify physical medium, emission origin and listener perspective where relevant. Stills cannot establish flight, acting, temporal continuity or sound.

## Turn criticism into direction

Assign each reference an explicit role: architecture/design, identity, pose, composition, material/light or camera behaviour. A request to improve photographic rendering does not authorise replacing a liked invented world with ordinary real-world architecture. Preserve the designed geometry/silhouette while changing the observed synthetic light/material cues. Shadow Apprentice v08 lost its preferred neo-futuristic design by misusing real-night photography as architectural authority; v09 restores the reference roles. Carry that decision lesson, not its particular rooftop design, into later films.

First name what must be preserved and why. Separate an observed defect, a taste judgment, an intentional impossibility and an untested hypothesis. Include a plausible counter-reading: for example, a reveal may be obvious because the opening already shows a creature-shaped ornament. Do not force negative findings when the inspected work serves the brief.

Choose the most consequential issue or opportunity. Describe the desired visible change, protected regions/invariants, actual reference dependencies and affected shots. Prefer a coherent design principle over more decoration. Change the smallest useful scope, which can still be a bold redesign when the world itself is bland. Distinguish essential coherence repairs from optional director variants. Avoid fixed scores, image quotas and arbitrary retry counts.

If execution is authorised, use the available image-generation/editing skill and inspected references. Preserve the prior output and attempt history; make a new review revision after a delivered pack. Correct the anchor/environment first when the defect would propagate. Inspect the result against the original at comparable scale, checking both the requested improvement and collateral changes. Propagate only a demonstrably stronger selected direction to affected frames. A text-only revision is not an image-complete revision.

Continue while a specific next attempt has a credible purpose. Stop/resume honestly on actual tool/account limits or repeated attempts with no meaningful progress; retain successful assets and explain the remaining issue. Native ImageGen is the Lab's default. An external-tool proposal needs an observed gap and current verified route/access/cost; it does not authorise paid submission. Keep frozen approved sources unchanged and place production derivatives outside them.

## Leave a compact evidence trail

Save a dated SELF_REVIEW.md in the active draft before its manifest is finalised. For an already delivered or approved packet, write a separate review sidecar outside that packet; do not invalidate its hashes. Use only as much structure as helps:

- Project/revision, scope, inspected assets and coverage limits.
- What to preserve; story surprise and visual-design judgment separately.
- Highest-impact findings, with asset/region evidence and why each matters.
- Selected improvement: visible change, protected elements, references, affected shots and executable edit direction.
- Actual before/after outcome, regressions, unresolved questions and next action. Label proposals as unexecuted.

Verify updated assets/links/hashes with the project's existing export checks. Agent inspection is not Jason's creative approval. Keep review alternatives out of the approved starting packet; carry relevant unresolved factual limitations into its production brief.

Learn from actual feedback: date and attribute the accepted/rejected component, compare retained examples, and update project ART_DIRECTION.md. Promote technical claims to LESSONS.md only with attributable inspected evidence, keeping hypotheses provisional. Amend this skill narrowly when real usage reveals a repeatable decision error, and validate it after changes; do not accumulate speculative restrictions.
