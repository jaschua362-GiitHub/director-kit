# craft/ · the director's required reading

Adopted in the lab on 12 November 2026, published in the kit on 25 November 2026.

## Why it exists

By November the lab's lessons filled about 95,000 words of retrospectives, briefs and notes. Directors (human and AI) stopped absorbing them: House of Courtesy repeated 18 faults that a written rule already covered, eight because the rule never reached the session's handoff and six because sessions ran past about 380 K of context and broke it anyway. So the lessons were rewritten by **when a director needs them**, not by the date they were learned.

## What a director reads

| When | Read | Size |
|---|---|---|
| Every session, first | [DIRECTOR_CRAFT.md](DIRECTOR_CRAFT.md) | about 900 words |
| Every session | [handoff-and-sessions](cards/handoff-and-sessions.md), [money-and-approvals](cards/money-and-approvals.md), [working-with-jason](cards/working-with-jason.md) | about 500 words each |
| At the start of a stage | That stage's card in [cards/](cards/) | about 500 words |
| At the start of a stage | The handoff written on [HANDOFF_FORM.md](HANDOFF_FORM.md) | one page |
| When choosing a route or technique | [TOOLBOX.md](TOOLBOX.md) | a menu, not required reading |

Stage cards: [story-and-prep](cards/story-and-prep.md), [board](cards/board.md), [plates](cards/plates.md), [pilot](cards/pilot.md), [production](cards/production.md), [fight-sequence](cards/fight-sequence.md), [cut](cards/cut.md), [sound](cards/sound.md), [vertical](cards/vertical.md), [title-and-poster](cards/title-and-poster.md), [closeout-and-social](cards/closeout-and-social.md).

"Jason" in the cards is the lab's creative lead. In your own work, read it as whoever owns the vision and the money: the person whose typed yes a paid job needs.

## How a card is built

Each card has the same parts: what the stage is for, what good looks like, numbered things to look for (each with the film that taught it), "Let the script check", how the stage runs, and what is settled. A line marked `tool: tools/craft_check.py …` is a check the script runs; one marked "lab script, not in kit" names a script from a lab film you can rebuild for your own project.

## Keeping it alive in your own lab

- A new lesson **replaces or merges a line**. No dated blocks; cards stay at about 500 words and the craft page at 900.
- The full story of a film goes in its retrospective, which is archive (see `../guides/`). The card gets one line.
- At every closeout, count the repeat faults: faults that a card line already covered. If the number is not falling, rewrite the line or move the check into a script.
- A lesson a script can check goes into a script, and comes off the director's reading.

## Not in the kit

The lab keeps a depth file behind each card (every lesson with its reason and source) and a ledger of about 800 merged lessons traced to their sources. Both quote private working notes and stay in the lab. Toolbox rows for methods the lab has studied but not yet tested are also held back until they have been tried on a film.
