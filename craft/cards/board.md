# Board card

Read this when you start the board stage. Required reading: `DIRECTOR_CRAFT.md`, the three every-session cards, this card and your handoff. The Lab keeps a longer depth file behind this card; it is not in the kit.

## What this stage is for
You propose the film here; Jason judges whether it is worth watching to the end. Music, titles and post are locked before spending.

## What good looks like
- The synopsis is a seed. Small Cargo's board followed it one to one: "the story and camera angle is not so exciting". Propose the better angle first: "It's like a partner."
- Framing lifts a plain script, as The Unstitched did: "a very boring, very plain script into a nicely narrated emotional piece".
- An arc: world, beauty, misdirection, reveal, reaction, payoff, closing beat; The Unstitched round 1 lacked one and failed.
- Contrast: a reverse, over-shoulder or motivated move, size jumps, one unseen shot; Small Cargo shot a scene from one side.

## Look for
1. Both formats framed per shot: lens, side, move, 9:16 window, crop or native vertical priced now; House of Courtesy's Reel lost this plan.
2. Reveals readable on a phone: cause shown, eyeline-led, held, then a reaction (The Unstitched, After You).
3. Twist and set piece on screen, each with its own shot (Shadow Apprentice scope POV, cloak orbit).
4. One action per shot: Evade and Escape's Kaede blended into the robots (three extra drafts).
5. Fights, contact and risk beats as start frames (a still the video starts from) or with a patch budget (Airborne); a whole fight film: `fight-sequence.md`.

## Let the script check
- Variety: static and same-side share, average and longest shot. Small Cargo's dull board: ten of 15 static or drifting, 2.5 s average. `not built: do it by hand`
- Climax position on the story runtime (title excluded) and the payoff's runtime, reported; no pass mark. `not built: do it by hand`

## How this stage runs
- Black-and-white pencil thumbnails; photoreal only after his lock: "dont waste credit on actual image gen". Prep art is a style guide.
- Before the first critique, deliver two boards, faithful and bold; name your pick.
- Critiques open with "would you watch this to the end, and which shot would you remember?" One outside critique round before every lock.
- One Artifact message: board, REQUIREMENTS_LOCK.md (every music, SFX, title, ending, deferred and spend row; effects with a 2 s local test) and a free rough preview with planned sound. No paid generation before every row has his answer; late answers cost Hold the Note 502 M tokens.
- Sessions and models: the handoff card.

## Settled
- One-prompt route (one generation shoots the film from a prompt): the approved timed-beat prompt is the board.
- Per-shot 9:16 framing and outside critique apply to every film.
- Climax position: report where it falls and how much runtime the payoff gets; no fixed pass mark. His 22 Oct ruling: such numbers "are learning evidence, not compulsory methods".
