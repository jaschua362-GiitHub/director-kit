# Title-and-poster card

Read this when you start the title-and-poster stage. Required reading: `DIRECTOR_CRAFT.md`, the three every-session cards, this card and your handoff. The Lab keeps a longer depth file behind this card; it is not in the kit.

## What this stage is for
It makes the end title, its sound, and the key art (the poster). Jason judges whether the title has wow and the key art shows the characters.

## What good looks like
- Title choreography and scale changes at natural speed. *The Unstitched: "one of my best experiences and wow factor for the video title".*
- A still title breathes with soft natural motion: "natural tea smoke so there are some animation going on". *House of Courtesy.*
- Title sound only from the film's own sounds, mellow, the cue carrying on under it. *Small Cargo: "abit too sharp ... as is in space".*
- Key art leads with the protagonist and signature figures, standing, facing camera so their design shows. *Small Cargo: "characters design is one of the keyart we would want to post in IG as main attraction".* An action film's wide poster is an action frame, face close (Neo Siam).

## Look for
1. Title centring on the letters' optical body, leaving out tails and splatter. *Wind the Man; recurring note.*
2. Key-art faces at 100 % against the approved plate, and anything the model added. *House of Courtesy: a glowing ring appeared.*
3. Open eyes on the poster face. *Hold the Note.*
4. The ending in the cut, fades on, at phone size. *One Beat Late: a dead ending.*

## Let the script check
- Last frames fully black and silent. `not built: do it by hand`
- Title centred in measured negative space, readable as a thumbnail. `not built: do it by hand`
- 9:16 cover's title inside Reel zones, the 4:5 grid and the 3:4 profile crop. `lab script, not in kit: compose_cover_safe.py`
- Subtitles at about 0.90 of frame height, clear of faces. `not built: do it by hand`
- 9:16 titles rendered natively at 1080x1920. `not built: do it by hand`

## How this stage runs
- Settle the title, black before it, any epigraph, the last image and sound in the lock before production. *Hold the Note: 502 M tokens against 80 M, largely from late decisions.*
- Ask in lock row 7 (the lock's title row) whether the title gets its own sound; otherwise land it on a sound already in the mix. *Small Cargo: "design a end title sound design".*
- Key art is designed: a text-free render, approved plate as face reference, face pixels composited back, title hand-set with `lab file, not in kit: title_lockups.py`. *One Beat Late: model lettering was "very amateur".*
- One designed still approved before title motion; show two or three lockups, he picks.
- Deliver 9:16 and 16:9 key art: "Need a wide 16x9 as YouTube needed it".
- Sessions and models: the handoff card.

## Settled
- Title sound: ask; silence is only the default.
- Key art: from the plate; the approved title artwork inside it is scaled only.
