# Closeout and social card

Read this when you start the closeout-and-social stage. Required reading: `DIRECTOR_CRAFT.md`, the three every-session cards, this card and your handoff. The Lab keeps a longer depth file behind this card; it is not in the kit.

## What this stage is for
The approved film becomes one delivery folder and posts that go out on time, verified. Jason judges the copy's voice and whether everything went out as approved.

## What good looks like
- Posts read as him talking: "i prefer in conversation sharing dont use points notes". Paragraphs, no numbered points.
- One voice per platform: LinkedIn, the process and tools, short; X, about three posts teaching video-gen artists; personal Facebook, his voice, story first, then a paragraph on Claude Code as director; Instagram and YouTube, the source story in two or three sentences.
- Carousel: a straight grid ("more structure layout not in slanted version"), one plain line per shot, panels from the locked board.

## Look for
1. The director writes the copy; a Sonnet helper packages the kit and posts from one written spec.
2. His yes on copy, alt text and posting order before any kit: "1 step before post kit artifacts i had to check n approve the post write up first" (Himmapan).
3. The kit as an Artifact link, rebuilt whenever finals change. Hold the Note's was local only: "could not locate hold the note posting kits".
4. Each post checked three minutes after its slot, reported unasked. Small Cargo's Reel failed at 18:00; he found it himself.
5. Release time checked against other films' scheduled posts (House of Courtesy: he had to ask). Default 18:00 local time; YouTube first, then Reel, then Story, each with the poster.

## Let the script check
- Delivery hashes, decode, index: `lab script, not in kit: publish_final_render.py` (via `lab file, not in kit: publish_with_bundled_ffmpeg.py`).
- Social materials: `lab script, not in kit: package_social_materials.py`.
- Instagram upload copy by `social/metricool/UPLOAD_ROUTE.md` (25 Mbps maximum, 500 MB; AAC 128 kbps published, 192 kbps proven); X posts under 280 weighted characters; no runtime in posts: not built: do it by hand.

## How this stage runs
- Deliver masters, art, end title, stills, 2x stills, posters and approved copy as one self-contained folder; he zips it himself.
- After copy and schedule are approved, schedule the Reel in Metricool at best quality with no second ask; stop and report if anything differs.
- Close with the debrief: save his remarks word for word, write `closeout/TOKEN_REPORT.md`, count the repeat faults (faults a card line already covered), and merge each new lesson into `DIRECTOR_CRAFT.md` or a card by replacing or merging a line: "i hope your critic can be share for all video director agent for next film". The film's full story goes in an archive retrospective, not required reading.
- A final film gets no more generation or edits; add its 24 h and 7 d audience row. Never edit frozen approved packets.

- Release order: YouTube, Reel, Story, not one slot.
