# Handoff and sessions card

Read this at the start of every session. Required reading: `DIRECTOR_CRAFT.md`, the three every-session cards, your stage card and your handoff. The Lab keeps a longer depth file behind this card; it is not in the kit.

## What this stage is for
Carry the film forward in as few hands as possible, losing no decision. Tokens are replies times context.

## What good looks like
- He hands the film over and it runs: "It's autonomous ... Concept, editing, cinematography is all handled well and even surprised me. That is the fun".
- Lessons travel whole: "Every round, every experience that we cumulative, the information is being diluted in the next film".

## Look for
1. A helper judging a take, cutting, or starting a session. Only the supervisor and the director start sessions. The Last Fritter chained fifteen sessions: 22 repeated faults, 968.9 M.
2. A handoff that does not name the next stage's card by path. No Fritter handoff named `production.md`; seven of 22 repeats sat there.
3. The director doing batch work itself. Fritter's director ran the builds: 517.7 M in one session.
4. Work reaching him that its director has not passed. No director saw Fritter between the story and the rough cut.
5. One session carrying takes, cut, vertical and HD. The Last Ferry's video session: 1,350 replies, context to 963 K, 524 M of the film's 792 M against a 140 M plan.

## Let the script check
- Peak context per session, in every gate message: `tool: tools/craft_check.py context "<film>"`.
- The handoff names the stage card; must-nots last, money and frozen files only: `not built: do it by hand`.

## How this stage runs
- **Fable 5.2, the taste gates.** Shapes the story with him and directs the board and the look gate itself, a fresh session per gate. Then it starts the director and looks once more, at the locked cut: one verdict, pass or the faults by shot.
- **Director, Opus 5.5 in high mode.** One director holds the film from the plates batch to the masters and certifies it: continuity sheet, plates, video prompts, take choice, the cut, sound, finish. "The person hang on to it all the way till we finish the video" (The Last Ferry).
- **Same director, fresh context.** The director restarts its own session from a one-page handoff on `craft/HANDOFF_FORM.md` at three points: draft takes chosen, cut locked, before the HD finish. Past about 250 K between those, restart at the next of them, never to a helper.
- **Helpers, Sonnet 5.5.** Renders, waits, progress checks, routine QC, measurements, page builds, ledger, posting: one written spec, back as pass or fail. Never the edit, a take choice, or anything he judges. Social runs in its own lower session after the masters.
- His gates: the story, the board, the look, the first full cut with sound, the final, and each money yes. He sees only what its director has passed, never a list of known flaws.
- The lock sets a token target for each role; report against it at every gate, and restate it when runtime, formats or the finish route change.
- The handoff opens with his 9 Nov mandate and a grant to restage. Status: the project's file and its `START_HERE.md` row; no dated blocks.
