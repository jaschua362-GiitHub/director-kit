# Pilot card

Read this when you start the pilot stage. Required reading: `DIRECTOR_CRAFT.md`, the three every-session cards, this card and your handoff. The Lab keeps a longer depth file behind this card; it is not in the kit.

## What this stage is for
The pilot takes the film's hardest needed shot into motion before the rest is bought. It proves the plates, the model route and the look survive movement. Jason judges whether the take could be final and whether the route is worth scaling.

## What good looks like
- A real shot from the film with the hard part in it: a moving face, a hand on a prop, a light change. A frozen face with a camera drift proves nothing.
- The still's full finish kept in motion; softened skin is not an acceptable default (31 Oct).

## Look for
1. Every frame from frame 1 to the last at 100 %, judged as the final: opening, middle and exit, not only the join. House of Courtesy B9's slipcase crossed her hand in frames 1 to 13 while the check covered the join; six takes, about USD 7.
2. The take at normal speed, as motion: weight, contact, action order. Thumbnails, contact sheets and metrics never pass a shot; a "near-still" note is a stop. House of Courtesy published B2 and B10 near-still: USD 3.96 in retakes.
3. Before the first paid take, the actual frames: rig, floor plan, screen sides, eyelines, prop states, costume asymmetry, light source. Solar Etiquette lost most retries skipping this.

## Let the script check
- Shadow timing: mid-shot luminance on the first take; the shadow arrives after its cause and never brightens again. `not built: do it by hand`
- Draft media downloaded at once; Atlas keeps it 7 days. `not built: do it by hand`

## How this stage runs
- First cut the plates into a free stills animatic at the board's timings and watch it with him. House of Courtesy's showed the tea-server missing from the opening, fixed for USD 0.55.
- Quote the take and get his typed yes; draft at 480p (about USD 0.134/s); show him the draft; run the HD only after his yes on that draft. A re-pasted sheet is not a Go: House of Courtesy B9 lost USD 2.39, and Atlas has no cancel.
- One model bake-off per film, on a shot with no person, picks the cheap model; Wan 3.0 won House of Courtesy's mechanism shot.
- After his notes, at most one re-roll, and only for global notes (pace, camera, tone), because a re-roll changes everything.
- The pilot is its own gate. Sessions and models: the handoff card.

## Settled
- A 480p draft is shown before every HD, even inside an approved batch; "Mini draft optional" is retired.
- Cheap models only on shots with no person in frame; faces and hands go to Seedance. Ask before extending.
