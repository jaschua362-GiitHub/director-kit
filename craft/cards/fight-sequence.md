# Fight-sequence card

Read this with the production and cut cards for a fight or fast action piece. From Neo Siam (TEST-028). More detail: `craft/depth/fight-sequence.md`; full story: `lab file, not in kit: DEBRIEF.md`.

## What this route is for
A fast action piece cut from a few long multi-shot takes, not from a board: "for this kind of fast action-packed anime style, I think this workflow kind of work for me". Story-driven films stay board-led.

## What good looks like
- "Do not be very safe ... It's an intense action video." Camera low and close, enemies filling the frame: "now it will be too clean".
- No break: mass enemy, a middle boss, the big bang. He scores drafts against his reference: "only 6 upon 10".
- The lead keeps her figure and face in every take: "not a big boy, a man fighting".

## Look for
1. Her build in the first second and in rear views. One 30 s take with nine references read "like a guy".
2. Seconds of two fighters trading blows, counted beside the last take he liked. d06's four hard-cut inserts lost a duel he rated "80%".
3. A humanoid middle boss: "a bit comical". An animal on four legs worked.

## Let the script check
- HD against its draft, and each join at 100 %: `not built: do it by hand`.

## How this route runs
- Before any video: a photoreal sheet of the lead (front, rear, running, profile, face, powered state, weapon) and a still of each place and enemy, small ones too ("more CGI" without one).
- Write the fight as timed beats, second by second, in Seedance 2.5 reference-to-video, split at the designed cuts into takes of 7 to 17 s.
- Close-ups are push-ins on a two-shot, never hard-cut inserts: each hard cut restages the place and the enemy.
- Change only what he named in a take he liked; put old and new side by side before sending.
- Cut at 480p until he locks it, then finish each used take once with draft-complete: frame numbers carry over.
- Two takes that will not cut: image-to-video, first frame the last frame of one, last frame the first of the next. A big gap cuts inside, so chain two halves. Take the anchors from HD frames; from 480p her face stays soft.
