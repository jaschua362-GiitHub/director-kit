# Story and prep card

Read this when you start preparing a film: it is for the preparation agent (synopsis and design references), not the video director, whose required reading is `DIRECTOR_CRAFT.md`, the three every-session cards and the handoff. The Lab keeps a longer depth file behind this card; it is not in the kit.

## What this stage is for
Give the director a story worth expanding and references it can trust. Jason judges concepts, then the design draft, by eye.

## What good looks like
- Give the director a premise, clear setup, visible change and payoff. For a story-led film, show the lead’s intention and choice. The director works out shots and timing.
- A one-line premise atop synopsis.md, a readable opening image, a middle question the viewer waits on (Hold the Note: will her breath hold?), and why viewers would share and follow; complete stories reached 10-30x the teasers.
- A beautiful female lead or sub-lead with a real role: "my film always have beautiful female lead or sub lead to pull audience in." Wind the Man's cast passed four reviews without one.
- When a legend or wisdom tradition informs the story, let the viewer meet it through the action and credit the source.
- Photoreal unless he names a look (Tide v03 looked CG). Benchmarks: The Unstitched, Hold the Note; Small Cargo is the dull one.

## Look for
1. A size line-up in the reference set (every figure and recurring prop standing together in one frame) and each prop shown in use on its figure. House of Courtesy had neither: the doll's size was undecided, the clamp shown only detached on a table; his notes on both took nine plate rounds and 230 M tokens.
2. Risk beats AI video does badly: crossings, objects in hands, readable text, reflections, asymmetric features, light changes, three actions in a row, high-angle faces, crowds. Airborne's drone kill and Hold the Note's shrinking flute failed.
3. A stated role for every reference. Shadow Apprentice v08 lost its world to night photos used as architecture.
4. Natural speech in the right register; for a culturally rooted world, that language (Korean for Hold the Note).

## Let the script check
- Each dialogue line through TTS then speech-to-text before the paid pilot (Hold the Note's 이을게). `not built: do it by hand`

## How this stage runs
- Monday at 10:00 local time is a roughly 10-second GenAI technique test. Define one filmmaking question, the action that reveals it and a visible pass criterion.
- Friday at 10:00 is one short film. Alternate meaningful stories with visually bold, audience-facing shorts that aim to land within about 30 seconds. The approaches can overlap. Sci-fi, historical fiction, mythology and wild retellings are all options. Establish a clear opening, action and payoff.
- Use the weekly creation references (lab file, not in kit: `references/storytelling/WEEKLY_CREATION.md`). Record the relevant source, the device it teaches and how the new idea transforms it. Bring three fresh options with a recommendation and wait for his selection.
- After selection, show a short written test brief, script or story foundation for discussion. Begin film design once he gives the story a clear go-ahead for visuals.
- After design lock, quote the photo render batch and wait for his yes.
- The story gate fixes the film's one format in lock row 1: native 9:16 by default, 16:9 only with his yes (25 Nov 2026; `vertical.md` § One format per film). The board is drawn for that format and priced against USD 35 (`money-and-approvals.md`).
- Self-review (cinematic-art-review, not his approval), then a local DRAFT marked awaiting_user_review, without video jobs or ZIP. Repeat runs resume, never regenerate.
- Pass every deferred item as an open question. Hold the Note's epigraph was closed by a "no poem or extra title" rule; both finals were redone.
- Frozen approved sources stay unchanged; derivatives go beside them.

## Settled
- Blocking maps and animatics are not preparation work.
- Fresh concepts each slot, not the saved slate.
