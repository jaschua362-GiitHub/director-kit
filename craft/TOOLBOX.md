# The director's toolbox

Techniques within reach, by what the film needs. Open this at the start of every film, when the route is chosen,
and again when a film hits a problem it names. It is a menu, not required reading: take a tool only when the
film's subject or type calls for it, and say in the handoff which ones you took and why.

**Proven** means a Lab film has used it. **Untested** means it was studied and kept, and the first film that uses
it is its test — tell Jason so when you propose it. Nothing here changes the hard limits: a quote and his typed
yes before any paid job.

## By what the film needs

| The film needs… | Tool | Status | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exact compositions, a locked board, a title choreography | Board and start-frame route | Proven | the stage cards |
| A chase, travel, action or mood sequence shot as one flowing take of up to 30 s | One-prompt takes | Proven (Airborne, Forsaken opener) | `skills/one-prompt-takes/SKILL.md` |
| A 15 s motion-graphics spot: a character reveal, title spot or pre-promotion, mostly type and flat colour with one figure | Graphic spot method | Proven once (FAI reveal, TEST-029) | `skills/graphic-spot-method/SKILL.md` |
| One location seen from many angles | Environment continuity (nine views) | Proven | `skills/environment-continuity/SKILL.md` |
| A 9:16 cut of a 16:9 film | Vertical reframe | Proven | `skills/vertical-reframe/SKILL.md` |
| The final grade and its checks | Colour grade | Proven | `skills/colour-grade/SKILL.md` |
| A self-review of concepts, references and stills | Cinematic art review | Proven | `skills/cinematic-art-review/SKILL.md` |
| Reference analysis, local editing and checks, a composition board | OpenMontage | Installed, optional | `integrations/openmontage/README.md` |

Routes combine. A main film is often a hybrid: one-prompt blocks for the flow, start-frame shots for the exact
moments.

## How to choose
1. Say what the film's subjects must **do**: hold a pose, move through space, act, speak, return next episode.
2. Find the rows that match. If an untested row matches, propose it to Jason as a test with its own quote; do
   not adopt it silently and do not skip it silently.
3. Write the choice in the handoff's "Route and toolbox" section, so the next session inherits it.

## Keeping this page
- A new technique earns a row when Jason says to keep it. One row, one line; the method lives in its own file.
- When an untested tool is used on a film, change its status here and record the result in the tool's own file.
